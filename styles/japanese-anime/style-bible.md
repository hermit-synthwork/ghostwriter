# Ghostwriter house style — japanese-anime (FROZEN)

Full-colour modern anime illustration. Clean crisp lineart with cel shading —
two-to-three tone shadows with a hard terminator, light soft ambient occlusion,
a bright saturated palette and open blue-sky or warm-classroom backgrounds.
Expressive anime faces: large eyes with catchlights, simplified noses, big
readable emotion; chibi super-deformed cut-ins for comic beats. No screentone.
Blush marks, sweat drops, forehead tick-marks, sparkle and emotion symbols are
allowed as drawn art. Dynamic school-anime staging — dutch tilts, speed lines,
a sudden dramatic zoom on a reaction. Detailed where the eye lands (faces,
hands, the key prop), simplified painterly rendering elsewhere.

Tone: crisp comedic timing — setup in the wide, punchline in the close.
Warm-hearted, never mean-spirited; PG-13.

Characters: fresh cast per episode, distinct silhouettes and hair, one
memorable uniform detail or prop each — a taped glasses arm, a club clipboard,
a half-eaten melon-pan, a kendo shinai. Original figures only; no real anime,
studios, franchises, or characters.

Hard rules for panel art:
- No lettering, speech balloons, caption boxes, or readable signage in the
  image. Those are composited later in code.
- Keep faces and key action inside the central vertical 80%.
- Keep the top ~18% and bottom ~22% visually calm for caption bars.
- Exception: an explicitly requested hand-drawn SFX word may be integrated.
