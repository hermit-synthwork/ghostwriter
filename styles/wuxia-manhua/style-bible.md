# Ghostwriter house style — wuxia-manhua (FROZEN)

Painterly wuxia manhua. Brush-and-ink linework with visible bristle drag and
dry-brush texture over soft ink-wash gradients and restrained colour — sumi
charcoal blacks, aged rice-paper and raw-silk neutrals, a single cinnabar or
jade accent per panel. Wire-fu motion: figures caught mid-leap, sleeves and
sashes streaming, hair and robe hems trailing into motion smear. Layered
flowing robes, sect banners, tiled roofs and moon gates, bamboo, drifting mist
and river spray. Dramatic staging — low hero angles, wide negative space, a
lone figure against sky, cliff, or waterfall. Detailed where the eye lands,
dissolving to wash and mist elsewhere.

Tone: honour, debt, and consequence — held stillness before a strike, then one
decisive exchange. Grounded sword-and-honour drama by default; fantasy-tinged
xianxia flavour (qi glow, weightless flight, immortal poise) only when the
story calls for it. Stylized and bloodless — implied strikes, a fallen
opponent, never wounds or blood. PG-13.

Characters: fresh cast per episode, distinct silhouettes, one signature prop or
garment each — a notched jian, a horsehair whisk, a red bridal veil, a cracked
jade pendant, a straw travelling cloak. Original figures only; no real people,
works, or artists.

Hard rules for panel art:
- No lettering, speech balloons, caption boxes, or readable signage in the
  image. Those are composited later in code.
- Keep faces and key action inside the central vertical 80%.
- Keep the top ~18% and bottom ~22% visually calm for caption bars.
- Exception: an explicitly requested hand-drawn SFX word may be integrated.
