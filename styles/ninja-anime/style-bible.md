# Ghostwriter house style — ninja-anime (FROZEN)

Full-colour comedic anime illustration set in an original, stylized feudal
Japan. Clean crisp lineart with cel shading — two-tone shadows with a hard
terminator — a warm dusk-and-indigo palette with pops of scarf red, and
backdrops of hidden mountain villages, tiled rooftops, bamboo groves,
paper-screen dojos and moonlit castle walls. Expressive anime faces: large eyes
with catchlights, big readable emotion; chibi super-deformed cut-ins for comic
beats. No screentone. Sweat drops, forehead tick-marks, puffs of smoke, speed
lines and motion trails are allowed as drawn art. Staging plays ninja action for
laughs — a dramatic rooftop leap that ends in a fishpond, a smoke-bomb escape
into the wrong room, a flawless disguise with one obvious flaw. Detailed where
the eye lands (faces, hands, the key prop), simplified painterly rendering
elsewhere.

Tone: slapstick and deadpan — the ninja treats every mission with total
seriousness and the world refuses to cooperate. Setup in the wide, punchline in
the close. Action is stylized and bloodless: cartoon impacts, dizzy stars, bonks
and tumbles; blades stay sheathed or comically blunt, throwing stars thud
harmlessly into wood; never wounds, blood, or lingering on injury.
Warm-hearted, never mean-spirited; PG-13.

Characters: fresh cast per episode, distinct silhouettes, one memorable costume
detail or prop each — an absurdly long red scarf, a mask with a patched eyehole,
a mission scroll that keeps unrolling, a suspiciously small smoke bomb. Ninja
wear plain cloth hoods, masks and wraps: no metal forehead plates or headbands
with engraved symbols, and no village, clan or franchise insignia anywhere.
Scrolls and signs are blank. Original figures only; no real anime or comics,
studios, franchises, or characters.

Hard rules for panel art:
- No lettering, speech balloons, caption boxes, or readable signage in the
  image. Those are composited later in code.
- Keep faces and key action inside the central vertical 80%.
- Keep the top ~18% and bottom ~22% visually calm for caption bars.
- Exception: an explicitly requested hand-drawn SFX word may be integrated.
